What is IPO Grey Market Premium (GMP)?

IPO Grey Market Premium (GMP) is the price at which IPO shares are traded unofficially before listing. It reflects market sentiment but is not regulated by SEBI and can be highly volatile.

Is IPO GMP reliable?

GMP depends on demand and supply in the grey market and may not accurately predict listing price or long-term performance.
