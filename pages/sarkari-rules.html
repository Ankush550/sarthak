🔴 LAGU HO GAYA 1 April 2026 se New Labour Rules Lagu!

India ki 29 purani labour laws ab officially 4 New Labour Codes mein merge ho gayi hain. Yeh rules 21 November 2025 ko notify hue aur 1 April 2026 se fully lagu ho gaye hain. Neeche padhen — kya badla aur aap par kya asar hoga.

💰
Rule 1: 50% Wage Rule
Sabse badi change — salary structure badla
NEW

Basic Pay + DA = minimum 50% of Total CTC

📌 Pehle kya tha: Companies basic pay 20-30% rakhti thi, baaki allowances mein dakar PF aur gratuity bachati thi.

✅ Ab kya hoga: Basic pay minimum 50% hogi — iska matlab aapka PF contribution, gratuity aur bonus sabhi badh jaayenge.

💡 Example: Agar CTC ₹40,000 hai toh basic ≥ ₹20,000 honi chahiye. Pehle ₹10,000 basic par PF ₹1,200/month tha — ab ₹2,400/month hoga!

Rule 2: Gratuity – 1 Saal Mein!
Fixed-term employees ke liye badi relief
BIG CHANGE

Fixed-term workers ko ab sirf 1 saal mein gratuity milegi

📌 Pehle: Gratuity ke liye 5 saal continuous service compulsory thi — contract workers ko kuch nahi milta tha.

✅ Ab: Fixed-term employees (contractual) ko pro-rata gratuity 1 saal baad milegi. 2 saal kaam kiya toh 2 saal ka gratuity milega.

💡 Example: 2 saal kaam kiya, last basic ₹20,000 → Gratuity = (20,000 × 15 × 2) ÷ 26 = ₹23,077

⚠️ Note: Permanent employees ke liye 5 saal ki limit abhi bhi laagu hai.

⚡
Rule 3: 48-Hour F&F Settlement
Resignation ke baad salary roke nahi!
NEW

Resignation ya termination ke 48 ghante mein poora hisaab barabar

📌 Pehle: Full & Final settlement mein hafte ya mahine lag jaate the. Companies salary rokti rahi thi.

✅ Ab: Company ko resign/dismiss/retrench hone ke 48 ghante mein yeh sab dena hoga:
→ Baaki salary | → Leave encashment | → PF settlement initiation | → Gratuity (agar applicable)

⚠️ Delay hone par company ko interest bhi dena hoga aur Labour Commissioner mein complaint ka haq milega.

📱
Rule 4: Gig Workers Coverage
Delivery, cab, freelance workers ab cover
HISTORIC

Swiggy, Zomato, Ola, Uber drivers aur delivery workers ab social security ke haqdar

📌 Pehle: Gig workers (app-based) kisi bhi labour law ke bahar the — koi benefit nahi.

✅ Ab milega:
→ Health insurance | → Maternity benefit | → Disability protection | → Retirement fund access

💰 Platform companies (Swiggy, Ola etc.) को अपने annual turnover का 1-2% Social Security Fund mein dena hoga

India mein 7.7 crore gig workers — pehli baar protected!

📅
Rule 5: 4-Day Work Week Option
Choice of employer aur employee
OPTIONAL

Hafte mein 4 din kaam — 3 din chhutti (lekin overtime rules saath mein)

✅ Company 4-day week offer kar sakti hai lekin:
→ Weekly hours same rahenge (48 hours)
→ Iska matlab: 4 din × 12 ghante kaam
→ Overtime: 8 ghante se zyada = double pay mandatory

⚠️ Yeh optional hai — employer aur employee dono ki consent chahiye. Company force nahi kar sakti.

📋
Rule 6: Appointment Letter + Women Rights
Naye worker protections
MANDATORY

Appointment Letter Compulsory: Ab har employer ko EVERY employee ko formal appointment letter dena hoga — chahe permanent ho ya contractual. Pehle sirf kuch sectors mein zaroori tha.

Women Night Shift (7PM-6AM): Women ab night shift kar sakti hain — lekin sirf apni written consent se. Employer ko safe transport aur secure workplace guarantee dena hoga.

📌 Appointment letter na milne par Labour Commissioner mein complaint kar sakte hain.

4 New Labour Codes 2026 – Kya Badla? Poori Jaankari
Wage Code, Industrial Relations, Social Security, Occupational Safety
NEW LAW +
⚠️ India ki 44 purani labour laws ko milaakar 4 naye Labour Codes banaaye gaye hain. Yeh sabhi states mein lagu hone wale hain — iska asar laakon workers par padega.
1
Code on Wages 2019 — Minimum wage, equal remuneration aur timely payment ka ek code. Har worker — permanent ya contractual — ko minimum wage milni chahiye. Gender discrimination band.
2
Industrial Relations Code 2020 — Strike, layoff, retrenchment ke naye rules. 300 se kam workers wali company ko government permission nahi chahiye layoff ke liye (pehle 100 thi).
3
Social Security Code 2020 — PF, Gratuity, ESIC, maternity benefit sab ek jagah. Gig workers aur platform workers bhi ab cover honge.
4
Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions Code 2020 — Kaam karne ki jagah ki safety, 8 ghante kaam, overtime ka double pay, women ko raat ki shift mein safety guarantee.

💡 Sabse Badi Change — "Floor Wage" Concept

New Wage Code mein ek National Floor Wage hogi — matlab koi bhi state is minimum se neeche nahi ja sakti. Abhi yeh approximately ₹178/day hai. Rajasthan, UP, Bihar, MP — sab states ko is se upar minimum wage rakhni hogi.

Provident Fund (PF) Rules 2026 – Calculation aur Withdrawal
EPF contribution, interest rate, withdrawal rules – sab kuch
UPDATED +
1
Contribution Rate: Employee 12% basic+DA deता है। Employer bhi 12% deta hai — lekin employer ke 12% mein se 8.33% EPS (pension) mein jaata hai aur baaki 3.67% EPF mein.
2
Interest Rate 2025-26: EPF par 8.25% per annum interest milta hai — yeh tax-free hai. Fixed deposit ya savings se kaafi zyada.
3
Withdrawal Rules: 5 saal ki service ke baad full withdrawal tax-free. Emergency mein partial withdrawal allowed — illness, marriage, home loan ke liye.
4
EPFO UAN: Har employee ka ek Universal Account Number hota hai. Job change par bhi yeh same rehta hai — PF transfer automatically hota hai.

Gratuity Rules 2026 – Formula, Eligibility aur Calculator
5 saal ki naukri baad kitni gratuity milegi – instantly calculate karein
MUST KNOW +

📐 Gratuity Ka Formula

Gratuity = (Last Basic Salary × 15 × Years of Service) ÷ 26

Jahan: Last Basic = aakhri mahine ki basic pay + DA | 15 = 15 din ki salary per year | 26 = ek mahine ke working days

✓
Eligibility: Sirf unhe milti hai jo ek company mein minimum 5 saal kaam kar chuke hain. Death ya disability hone par 5 saal ki limit nahi.
₹
Maximum Limit: Government employees ke liye gratuity limit ₹25 lakh tak tax-free hai. Private employees ke liye bhi ₹20 lakh tak tax-free hai (7th Pay Commission ke baad).
⏰
Payment Deadline: Company ko resign/retirement ke 30 din ke andar gratuity deni hogi. Delay hone par interest bhi dena hoga.

ESIC Rules 2026 – Medical Benefits, Sickness Benefit, Calculator
Employee State Insurance – kya milta hai, kitna contribution
UPDATED +
1
Contribution: Employee 0.75% of gross salary deta hai। Employer 3.25% deta hai। ₹21,000/month se kam salary wale employees ESIC mein enrolled hona chahiye.
2
Medical Benefit: Employee aur poori family ko ESIC dispensary aur hospitals mein free OPD, IPD, medicine, surgery milti hai — koi limit nahi.
3
Sickness Benefit: Bimari ke dino mein average daily wage ka 70% cash benefit milta hai — max 91 din per year। Extended sickness mein 2 saal tak 80%.
4
Maternity Benefit under ESIC: Full pay par 26 hafte. Miscarriage ke liye 6 hafte. Medical bonus ₹5000 bhi milta hai.
