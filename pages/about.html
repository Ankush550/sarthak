ℹ️ Hamare Baare Mein

SarthakYojana.in ek free informational platform hai jo aapko sarkari yojanaon aur naukri ki puri jaankari deta hai — bilkul simple Hindi mein.

🌐 Hum Kaun Hain?

SarthakYojana.in ek swatantra (independent) digital platform hai jo bharat ke aam nagrikon ke liye sarkari yojanaon, naukri bhartiyon, results, admit cards aur documents se judi sahi aur updated jaankari ek jagah provide karta hai.

Hamara maksad yahi hai ki har vyakti — chahe wo kisi bhi gaon, kasba ya sheher mein ho — sarkari suvidhaon ki jaankari aasaani se apni bhasha mein pa sake. Bahut se log government schemes ka labh nahi le paate kyunki unhe pata hi nahi hota. Hum yahi khaai poorni karte hain.

🎯 Hamara Mission

Hamara mission hai — "Sahi Jaankari, Sahi Samay Pe."

Hum chahte hain ki India ka har nagarik — kisan ho, student ho, mazdoor ho ya grihini — har sarkari yojana ka labh le sake. Isliye hum yojanaon ki jaankari aur apply karne ki prakriya ko dum simple Hindi mein samjhaate hain.

💡 Hamari Core Values

✅ Shudhta (Accuracy)

Hum sirf verified aur official sources se jaankari lete hain. Koi bhi galat ya bhraamak jaankari share nahi karte.

🆓 Muft Seva (Free Service)

Hamara poora platform bilkul free hai. Kisi bhi jaankari ke liye koi charge nahi liya jaata.

⚡ Samayik (Timely)

Naukri bharti, result, admit card — sab kuch latest updates ke saath daily update kiya jaata hai.

🇮🇳 Desh Ke Liye

Hum kisi political party ya government department se connected nahi hain. Hum sirf aapke liye kaam karte hain.

📋 Hum Kya Provide Karte Hain?

SarthakYojana.in pe aapko milega:

💼 Latest Jobs — SSC, Railway, Police, Army, State PSC, Bank aur sabhi sarkari bharti ki puri jaankari.

📋 Sarkari Yojana — PM Kisan, Ujjwala, Ayushman Bharat, Ladli Behna aur 500+ yojanaon ki details.

📊 Results — Board exams, sarkari bharti results, cut-off marks.

🎟️ Admit Card — Exam admit cards download links directly.

📄 Documents — Aadhar, PAN, Ration Card, Domicile Certificate kaise banaye.

🎓 Education — Scholarship, admission forms aur university updates.

⚠️ Zaroori Suchna

SarthakYojana.in ek swatantra informational website hai. Yeh kisi bhi sarkari vibhag, ministry ya government body ka official portal NAHI hai. Hamari website pe di gayi jaankari sirf awareness ke uddeshya se di jaati hai.

Kisi bhi yojana mein apply karne ke liye hamesha official government website ka hi upyog karein. Hum kisi bhi kaam ke liye fees NAHI lete. Agar koi aapke is platform ka naam lekar paisa maange, toh seedha police ko report karein.
