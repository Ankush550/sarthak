Who We Are

SarthakYojana.in is a free, independent online information portal dedicated to helping job seekers across India stay updated with the latest government job notifications, exam results, admit cards, answer keys, and government welfare schemes (Sarkari Yojana). We were founded in 2022 with a simple mission — to make government job information easily accessible to every Indian, regardless of where they live or what device they use.

The name "Sarthak" means meaningful or purposeful in Hindi, and "Yojana" means plan or scheme. Together, SarthakYojana represents our commitment to providing purposeful, meaningful career and welfare information that helps citizens of India make informed decisions about their lives and futures.

We are based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and our small but dedicated team works round-the-clock to monitor official government websites, PSU portals, recruitment boards, and news sources so that our readers receive accurate and timely information — often within hours of official notifications being released.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the information gap between government recruitment bodies and the millions of job seekers across India, especially those in semi-urban and rural areas who may not have easy access to multiple news sources. We believe that every eligible candidate in India deserves equal access to government job information — and SarthakYojana.in is our way of making that happen.

What We Cover

SarthakYojana.in covers a comprehensive range of government career and welfare content:

  • Sarkari Naukri (Government Jobs): UPSC, SSC, IBPS, RRB, State PSC, Defence, Police, PSU, Teaching, Banking, and more — from 10th pass to postgraduate level.
  • Exam Results: Results for all major government examinations as soon as they are officially declared.
  • Admit Cards: Hall ticket download links for upcoming government exams across India.
  • Answer Keys: Official and unofficial answer keys for government competitive examinations.
  • Government Schemes (Sarkari Yojana): Central and State government welfare schemes for farmers, women, students, senior citizens, and underprivileged citizens — including eligibility, benefits, and how to apply.
  • Private Jobs: Private sector job notifications for readers looking beyond government employment.

Our Values

Accuracy First

Every piece of information published on SarthakYojana.in is sourced directly from official government websites and verified before publishing. We never guess or speculate.

Always Free

SarthakYojana.in has been and will always remain completely free for users. We do not charge for job alerts, notifications, or any information on this site.

Daily Updates

Our team monitors official portals daily including ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, ibps.in, rbi.org.in, and all State PSC websites to bring you the freshest updates every day.

Reader-First

Every page on our site is designed with the job seeker in mind — clear layouts, easy-to-read tables, direct links, and mobile-friendly design for users on any device.

Transparency

We clearly state that SarthakYojana.in is an information portal and not an official recruitment agency. We always link directly to official sources so users can verify information themselves.

No Fraud

We strongly warn against recruitment fraud. SarthakYojana.in never asks for money for job placement. All official apply links on our site go directly to government portals.
