✅ Can NRI Apply for Govt Jobs?

Yes! Most Indian Government jobs are open to Indian citizens living abroad. As long as you hold an Indian passport or OCI card (for some positions), you can apply online from any country. The exam must be given in India.

  • UPSC IAS/IPS — Open to Indian citizens
  • PSU Jobs (ONGC, NTPC, BHEL) — Open
  • Banking (SBI, PNB, BOB) — Open
  • SSC CGL/CHSL — Open to Indian citizens
  • Defence (NDA, CDS) — Only Indian citizens

📋 Documents NRI Need to Apply

  • Valid Indian Passport (not expired)
  • OCI Card (if applicable)
  • Aadhaar Card (mandatory for most)
  • Indian bank account for fee payment
  • Educational certificates (attested)
  • Category certificate if SC/ST/OBC
  • Address proof in India (family home)

💰 How to Pay Application Fee from Abroad

  • Indian SBI/HDFC/ICICI international debit card
  • International credit card (Visa/Mastercard)
  • Ask family in India to pay on your behalf
  • Indian bank net banking (online)
  • UPI through Indian mobile number

✈️ Exam in India — Planning Your Trip

All government exams take place only in India. Plan your visit keeping these in mind:

  • UPSC Prelims — June | Mains — September
  • SSC exams — throughout the year
  • IBPS — October-November
  • Book flights 3-4 months in advance
  • Stay duration: 1-2 weeks minimum

Government Jobs in India for NRI 2026 — Complete Guide

Every year, thousands of Non-Resident Indians (NRI) and Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) consider returning to India for stable government employment. India's government job sector offers unmatched job security, competitive salaries with regular increments, pension benefits, medical coverage, and social prestige. SarthakYojana.in is India's trusted platform for NRI candidates seeking information about Indian government jobs from abroad.

UPSC Jobs for NRI 2026

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts India's most prestigious examinations including IAS, IPS, IFS, CDS, and NDA. Indian citizens living abroad with a valid Indian passport can appear for UPSC examinations. The Civil Services Examination (CSE) is held annually with Prelims in June and Mains in September. NRI aspirants must plan their India visit accordingly and ensure they meet all age and educational eligibility criteria.

PSU Jobs for NRI 2026

India's Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) like ONGC, NTPC, BHEL, IOCL, BPCL, GAIL, and SAIL offer excellent career opportunities for engineering and MBA graduates. Most PSU recruitments through GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) are open to Indian citizens worldwide. PSU salaries range from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 2,00,000+ per month (approximately USD 600 to USD 2,400+).

Banking Jobs for NRI 2026

India's nationalised banks and private banks recruit thousands of officers and clerks every year through IBPS (Institute of Banking Personnel Selection), SBI (State Bank of India), and individual bank notifications. These positions offer excellent starting salaries of Rs. 35,000-60,000 per month with perks, housing loan facilities, and career growth within the banking sector.

How NRI Can Apply for Indian Govt Jobs from USA

The process is entirely online. Visit the official recruitment website, register with your Indian mobile number and email, fill the application form, pay the fee using an international credit/debit card or through a family member in India, and submit. Download and print the application form for records. For examinations, you must physically appear in India at the designated exam centre.
