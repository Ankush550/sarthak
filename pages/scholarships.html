What is a Scholarship?

A scholarship is financial support given to students to help them continue their education without being held back by cost. In India, such support comes from the central and state governments as well as from private companies and trusts as part of employee welfare or corporate social responsibility programmes. This page lists both government and private scholarships available to students in 2026.

General Steps to Apply for a Scholarship

  • Read the eligibility conditions of the specific scholarship carefully
  • Collect the required documents such as Aadhaar, marksheets and income certificate in advance
  • Apply through the mode specified — online portal, official website, or through your institution/employer's HR department
  • Submit the form well before the last date along with self-attested documents
  • Track your application status and respond promptly to any verification requests

Documents Commonly Needed

Most scholarships ask for an Aadhaar card, recent passport-size photograph, previous year's marksheet, income certificate, bonafide/admission certificate from your institution, and a bank passbook copy for the amount to be credited.
