Privacy Policy – SarthakYojana.in

Last Updated: April 2026  |  Effective Date: January 2022

At SarthakYojana.in, we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website sarthakyojana.in. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please discontinue use of this website.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you use our Contact Form, you may provide us with personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and message content. This information is used solely to respond to your query and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

b) Automatically Collected Information

When you visit SarthakYojana.in, certain information is automatically collected by our web servers and analytics tools, including:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographic location (city/state level)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths on the website
  • Referring website URL (where you came from)
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)

This data is collected in aggregated, anonymised form for website analytics purposes and does not personally identify you.

c) Cookies and Tracking Technologies

SarthakYojana.in uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website (anonymised data).
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements based on your browsing behaviour. Google uses the DoubleClick cookie and similar technologies for this purpose.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the SarthakYojana.in website
  • To respond to your queries and contact form submissions
  • To send job alerts via WhatsApp/email if you have subscribed (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To analyse website traffic and user behaviour to improve content
  • To display personalised advertisements through Google AdSense
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

SarthakYojana.in uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on users' prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-8602963796651751) to serve advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to show relevant ads to users based on their browsing history and interests.

How to manage ad preferences:

  • Opt out of personalised advertising: Google Ad Settings
  • Opt out of DoubleClick cookie: Google Ad Personalization Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out: NAI Opt-out Page
  • About Google Ads: Google Advertising Technologies

Ads on SarthakYojana.in are clearly labeled as "Advertisements" and are served by Google AdSense. We do not control which specific ads are displayed — they are served automatically by Google based on page content and user interests.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyse website traffic. Google Analytics collects anonymised data about how visitors use our website. This data helps us understand which pages are most visited, where visitors come from, and how to improve user experience. Google Analytics does not collect personally identifiable information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Third-Party Links

SarthakYojana.in contains links to external websites including official government job portals, PSU websites, and other resources. When you click on these links, you leave our website and are subject to the privacy policies of those third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any external websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

SarthakYojana.in does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Our website is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older (as we publish content related to government job recruitment which has age eligibility criteria). If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If you have subscribed to job alerts, you can unsubscribe at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@sarthakyojana.in.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Your continued use of SarthakYojana.in after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
