What are Sarkari Yojana?

Sarkari Yojana refers to government welfare schemes launched by the Central or State governments of India for various sections of society. These schemes aim to provide financial assistance, healthcare, housing, education, and employment support to eligible citizens. Understanding government schemes helps you claim your entitled benefits.

How to Check Your Eligibility for Government Schemes

  • Visit the official scheme portal or myScheme portal at myscheme.gov.in
  • Check income, age, category, and occupation criteria for each scheme
  • Gather required documents (Aadhaar, income certificate, bank details)
  • Apply online or at your nearest CSC (Common Service Centre)
  • Call helpline numbers for scheme-specific assistance

Documents Generally Required

Most government schemes require: Aadhaar Card, bank account passbook, income certificate, residence proof, caste certificate (if applicable), and passport-size photographs.
