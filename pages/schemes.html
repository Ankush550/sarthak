Government Schemes 2026 – Sarkari Yojana

What are Sarkari Yojana?

Sarkari Yojana refers to government welfare schemes launched by the Central or State governments for various sections of society including farmers, women, students, elderly citizens, and the poor.

How to Check Eligibility

  • Visit the official scheme portal or PM India website
  • Check income, age, category and occupation criteria
  • Gather required documents (Aadhaar, income cert, bank details)
  • Apply online or at your nearest CSC centre
