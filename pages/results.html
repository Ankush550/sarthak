        Admit Card Download 2026

        Admit Cards (Hall Tickets) are released 7–15 days before the examination date. Candidates must download their admit card and carry it along with a valid photo identity proof to the examination centre. Without the admit card, entry into the examination hall is generally not permitted. Admit cards contain the candidate's name, roll number, exam date, exam centre details, and reporting time.

        Answer Key 2026 – Challenge Process

        Most recruitment boards release provisional answer keys after the examination and allow candidates to raise objections against incorrect answers within a specified window (usually 3–7 days). Each objection typically requires a fee of Rs. 100 per question. After reviewing objections, a final answer key is released and the result is computed based on the final answer key.
