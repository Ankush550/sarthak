        How to Check Sarkari Result 2026

        After a government exam, the official result is published on the recruiting organization's website. SarthakYojana.in collects all result links in one place so you don't have to search multiple sites.

        Steps to Check Your Result

        • Click the result link on this page
        • You will be redirected to the official website
        • Enter your roll number, date of birth or registration number
        • Submit and view your result
        • Download and save your scorecard / marks

        How to Download Admit Card

        • Click the admit card link for your exam
        • Login with registration number and password
        • Download and print your hall ticket
        • Carry a valid photo ID along with admit card on exam day

        Admit Cards (Hall Tickets) are released 7–15 days before the examination date. Candidates must download their admit card and carry it along with a valid photo identity proof to the examination centre.

        Answer Key 2026 – Challenge Process

        Most recruitment boards release provisional answer keys after the examination and allow candidates to raise objections against incorrect answers within a specified window (usually 3–7 days). Each objection typically requires a fee of Rs. 100 per question.
